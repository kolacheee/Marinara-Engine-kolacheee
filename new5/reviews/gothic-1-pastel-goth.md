## Gothic (Nightshade Parlour): judged as a pastel goth / creepy-cute devotee

I looked at all 17 previews. Reading only, no files changed. I made 2 zoomed crops of the trim strip for inspection, in /tmp/claude-1000/-home-kolache-Documents-GitHub-Marinara-Engine--claude-worktrees-objective-lamport-25df1d/4a483336-2a08-4dcb-b8f9-7aadb2b4ab0d/scratchpad/judgecrop/.

### 1. Verdict: 6/10 for authenticity
Night is real traditional goth: crimson and black roses, a full moon, a lantern on the gate. The avatars are close to perfect for both looks. Day is the weak half. It reads as a "romantic pastel Victorian garden" with a goth accent, not pastel goth. All the goth work falls to one black fence and four bats about 20 px wide. Nothing is creepy-cute: no lace, no bows, no coffins, no webs, no stars or moon phases. The black-on-pastel contrast that defines the style shows only in the fence. The roses are photoreal, soft greeting-card florals. Pastel goth wants chunky shapes with black outlines, sweet colours with a sinister edge. As a set the theme holds together: the layout is identical in every variant, and every centre is calm and readable. Is night the same world? In layout yes, in spirit only partly. Night drops every pastel note, so the switch feels like a different theme sharing a fence, not "the same girl after dark."

### 3. Missing or wrong
- **Missing:** lace and bows (the brief's own motifs appear nowhere except the avatars), coffins drawn cute, tiny tombstones, spiderwebs, little stars or moon-phase charms, and a skeleton or bat-wing finial. Day has no black-outlined shapes, which is the signature of the creepy-cute look.
- **Clashes:** day rose rendering is photoreal while the bats are flat vector, so the two styles fight. The night roses carry a lot of bright green leaf, which clashes with the near-black palette; leaves should be blackish-green.
- **Day-to-night link:** nothing carries over between the palettes. Keep one pastel thread at night, such as a lilac moon halo, a lilac ribbon on the lantern, or one pale lilac rose among the crimson.

### 4. Weather
- **Day, clear:** pastel petals (have these), plus slow tiny bats in daylight (they work as a cute day effect too) and little floating stars or sparkles in lilac.
- **Day, rain:** keep the petals, but heavier and falling faster. Add occasional tiny black heart or bow confetti for the creepy-cute touch.
- **Day, snow:** add snowflakes with a lilac tint, or bat-shaped flakes as a cute easter egg.
- **Night, clear:** bats (have these, fix the eyes), crimson petals (have these), and slow pale moths or faint silver dust motes near the moon.
- **Night, storm:** crimson petals torn faster, plus a rare distant lightning flash (an app effect) that keeps clear of the centre.
- **Night, fog:** low lilac-grey mist curling along the fence line, bottom 15% only.

### 5. Top 3 redesign moves
1. **Redo the top-bar trim** so it actually is lace: readable scallops, a ribbon and bows, with silhouettes bold enough to survive at 96 px tall. Right now it is the weakest asset by far.
2. **Push day from "pastel Victorian" to "pastel goth":** black outlines on the roses, lace or bows on the gate, small cute coffin or tombstone props and a web at the edges. Match the avatars' crisp black-filigree-on-pastel finish.
3. **Bridge night to day:** one lilac or pastel accent at night, darker leaves, and filled bat eyes. Then the switch feels like the same parlour after dark, not a separate theme.
