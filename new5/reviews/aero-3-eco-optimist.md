## Aero Lagoon: curator's verdict

### 1. Verdict: 5.5/10 for authenticity

The palette and the materials are right: sky blue to aqua, lime-green gloss, and soap bubbles with rainbow rims are real Frutiger Aero. It holds together well across all states, because the composition stays identical in each variant, and every centre is very calm and usable. But it reads as **sterile clip-art Aero, not the living eco-tech optimism I collect.** Nothing in it is alive. There is no grass texture, no underwater layer, no dew, no koi, no butterflies, no shafts of sunlight through water. The hills are plain smooth green humps with no blades or flowers. The lagoon is a flat surface we only ever see from above. The bubbles sit in two rigid, mirrored columns like a UI loading pattern rather than drifting naturally. It is the Windows Vista colour wheel without the Vista "Aurora" or wildlife-photo soul. As a calm chat background it works; as a love letter to the aesthetic it is only half-written.

### 2. Per image

- **backdrop-day: TWEAK.** Correct palette and a clean centre. Changes:
  - stagger the bubbles in size and position so the two sides are not mirror images;
  - give the hills real lush grass blades with dew glints and a few small white or yellow flowers at their bases;
  - add a split-level waterline at the bottom corners showing clear water below, with light caustics and a koi or a small fish shoal at the extreme bottom edge;
  - add soft god-rays from the upper right.
- **backdrop-night: KEEP (minor tweak).** The aurora plus glowing bubbles is lovely and very Vista-Aurora. Optional: a few faint bioluminescent glints or fireflies at the hill bases.
- **backdrop-overcast-day: TWEAK.** It goes too grey and lifeless: the hills turn olive and the mood dies. Keep it fresher:
  - a pearly blue-white sky with a faint silver brightness behind the clouds;
  - vivid wet emerald hills with clear dewdrops beaded on the grass tips.
  - The rain rings are good.
- **backdrop-overcast-night: KEEP.** Dark, calm and readable, and the glowing bubbles carry it. Faint rain rings would help (they are barely visible).
- **backdrop-snow-day: KEEP.** The frosty bubbles with ice crystals and the cracked turquoise ice are charming and on-theme.
- **backdrop-phone-day: TWEAK.** The bubbles form a perfectly symmetric ladder of four pairs, which looks like UI dots. Randomise their sizes and positions and add grass detail.
- **backdrop-phone-night: KEEP.** Same note on the symmetric bubbles.
- **backdrop-phone-overcast-day: TWEAK.** It does not match the desktop version:
  - it has puffy cumulus clouds where the desktop has an even overcast layer;
  - the sky reads as a dull slate grey.
  Match the desktop and brighten the sky to a pearl tone.
- **wx-leaves: REDO.** These are lime gel capsules or jelly beans, not leaves. There are no veins and no stems, and the round one is just a green ball. Redo them as glossy leaves with a visible midrib and veins, a short stem and a little translucency. Mix in one dewdrop-beaded leaf and one maple-ish or ginkgo shape.
- **avatar-char: KEEP.** The aqua orb with a leaf inside is textbook Aero.
- **avatar-user: KEEP.** The lime orb with a water droplet and ripple pairs well with the character avatar.

**Not in the set:**

- **phone-overcast-night**;
- **phone-snow**.

Desktop and phone coverage is incomplete. The trim is the biggest gap.

### 3. Missing or wrong

- **Missing life.** I expect koi or tropical fish, a butterfly or two (blue morpho or a swallowtail at the edges), dewdrops on blades of grass, and dandelion seeds. The prompt explicitly banned animals and fish, and that ban is what made this sterile. Allow small edge creatures.
- **Missing the underwater half-and-half shot.** This is the signature Aero composition: the camera at the waterline, sky above, a clear caustic-lit world below with bubbles rising from the depths. It can live in the bottom corners without touching the centre.
- **Grass is plastic.** Real Aero grass is photographic and lush, with backlit blades and bokeh. Here it is smooth gel.
- **Sunbeams.** There is a lens flare, but no volumetric rays through cloud or water.
- **Clashes:**
  - mirrored, evenly spaced bubble columns: too designed, not organic;
  - leaves that look like pills;
  - an overcast day that loses the optimism the aesthetic depends on (even Aero rain is fresh and dewy, never gloomy).

### 4. Weather

**Day, clear**
- **Rising bubbles:** transparent spheres with white specular arcs and rainbow rims, drifting upward and wobbling, in sizes 3x apart.
- **Lens sparkles:** soft white four-point glints that fade in and out.

**Day, breezy**
- **Glossy leaves** (fixed as above), tumbling slowly.
- **Dandelion seeds:** white wisps.

**Rain**
- **Dewdrops:** clear, refractive beads with a bright highlight, sliding down rather than rain streaks.
- The fresh rain-ring feel should carry over.

**Snow**
- **Frosted bubble-flakes:** small ice-glass orbs or crystalline six-point flakes with an aqua tint and a gloss highlight.

**Night**
- **Glowing aqua bubbles** (#4fe3ff, #9cffd8) with a soft halo.
- **Fireflies or bioluminescent motes:** tiny green-gold glows that pulse.
- **Occasional aurora shimmer specks.**

**Spring/summer variant**
- **Butterflies:** small blue morpho sprites, edge-only flutter.

### 5. Top 3 redesign moves

1. **Bring it to life at the edges.** Allow fish, koi and butterflies in the corner and edge zones. Add a split-level underwater view in the bottom corners with caustics, rising bubbles and a koi or fish shoal. Give the hills lush, backlit grass with dewdrops and tiny flowers. The centre stays calm.
2. **Break the symmetry.** Randomise bubble sizes, clusters and heights so they drift naturally rather than forming mirrored columns, on every backdrop and especially the phone ones.
3. **Fix the props and fill the gaps.** Redo wx-leaves as real veined glossy leaves. Make a topbar-band, for example a glossy aqua glass tube with tiny bubbles and a leaf sprig, or a chrome-and-water ripple strip. Add phone overcast-night and phone snow. Keep rainy days dewy and bright, not grey.
