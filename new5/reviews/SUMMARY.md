# Batch 1 fan panel: summary (2026-11-09)

Each theme was judged by three judges who are fans of its look. Each judge saw every batch-1 image of
the theme plus its prompts. The full reports are in this folder (`<theme>-<n>-<persona>.md`). The
batch-1 images themselves were deleted at the owner's request, so the redesign starts from these
written verdicts and not from the old pictures.

## Agreed by all judges

- Every centre was calm and readable, and every variant kept the same layout. Keep both.
- The judges asked for variants that batch 1 never requested: phone overcast-night, phone snow and a
  snow-night backdrop. The designer decides whether the app needs them.

## Per theme (where at least two judges agreed)

**Aero.**
- Too sterile and symmetric. The bubbles sit in mirrored columns, and the hills are untextured gel.
- Add life at the edges: dewy grass, light rays, an above/below-waterline view with caustics, and
  koi or butterflies (the old prompt banned animals).
- `wx-leaves` came out as jelly beans. Redo them as veined glossy leaves.
- Add a `wx-bubbles` sheet. Bubbles are the signature Aero particle.
- Add a glossy glass topbar band (Aero had none).
- The phone overcast image kept puffy cumulus clouds. The overcast days should stay bright and dewy,
  not drab.

**Gothic.**
- The lace trim failed. I measured it at full size: only a 12 px band in rows 45 to 57 of 96, with no
  scallops, ribbon or motifs. Redo it, or draw it in code.
- Day reads as a pastel Victorian garden, not pastel goth. Add black outlines and creepy-cute props
  (bows, lace, a little coffin or web).
- Night reads as a florist's window. Add stone arches, ivy, urns and fog at the edges.
- Split the bats: cute for day and eyeless silhouettes for night. Never leave transparent eye holes.
- Remove the bats from the rain and snow days. Add moths and fog wisps.
- The avatars and petals are excellent.

**Nautical.**
- It is not gouache: the day images are smooth digital and the storms are photoreal.
- It is not a harbour. Add a quay, a bollard, buoys, blank code-flag bunting and cottages.
- No brass appears in any backdrop.
- The rope trim came out as grey wire, thin and misaligned between day and night. Redo it as a fat tan
  hemp pair in the same position.
- The night beams cross the text zone (badly on phone). Remove the sailboat from the storms.
- No weather sheets were made. Wanted: gulls, spray, sea mist and fog.

**Steam.**
- The night lamps are electric filament bulbs. Make them gas lanterns.
- The gears connect to nothing. Add shafts, needles, flanges and one drive element, and cut the gear
  count.
- The `wx-gears` sheet is not weather. Replace it with soot smuts, steam wisps, embers and smog puffs.
- Topbar collars should become bolted, riveted flanges.
- Add cast iron, leather and engraved ornament, plus more sky depth or a mooring mast.

**Noir.**
- The night images are the real thing.
- The day images are flat and photoreal with no chiaroscuro. Add hard light, venetian-blind slats and
  a near-monochrome palette.
- The street reads as an open plaza. Make it a narrow canyon with an office-above-the-bar story anchor.
- The rain variants have icicles. Make them water drips.
- The overcast-night horizon is too hot.
- `wx-scraps` look like beige parchment. Make them grey, damp newsprint.
- The girder trim needs bigger rivets and a visible sodium edge.
- Add fog/steam and ember sheets.
