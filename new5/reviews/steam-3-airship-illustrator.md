## Steam (Brassworks Sky): judge report

Persona: an illustrator of airship-adventure fiction.

**Files reviewed (13):** backdrop-day, -night, -overcast-day, -overcast-night, -snow-day, -phone-day, -phone-night, -phone-overcast-day, topbar-band-day, -night, wx-gears, avatar-char, avatar-user.

There is no desktop snow-night, phone overcast-night, phone snow-day or phone snow-night. The prompts never asked for them, so the set is incomplete.

### 1. Verdict: 6.5/10

The materials are right: honey brass, copper with verdigris, blank gauges, a glass-domed conservatory and a smoky chimney skyline. The big dirigible top-left is a lovely object: a ribbed canvas envelope, a gondola with a promenade rail and a pusher prop. The night set is the strongest. The gas lamps and the warmly lit gondola windows feel like a story is about to start.

But this is a steampunk *wallpaper*, not an airship *adventure*. The sky is a flat parchment wash with no depth, no cloud banks and no sense of altitude. We stand on the rooftops looking at a ceiling, not out across a sky we want to sail. The corner gear stacks are the stock "steampunk = gears" shorthand and they hog both corners. The romance is in the ships and the light. The gears are set dressing. The centre is calm and very readable everywhere, which is the one thing it does perfectly.

### 2. Per image

- **backdrop-day: TWEAK.** The centre is fine but lifeless. Add a soft, low-contrast cloud bank or haze layers near the horizon (y 70-80%) and a slight warm-to-cool sky gradient for depth. Move the large airship about 20 px right so its tail fin is not clipped by the left edge.
- **backdrop-night: KEEP.** The gaslight pools, lit gondola and lit attic windows are exactly right. Optional: one faint searchlight cone angled up from a corner, outside the calm zone.
- **backdrop-overcast-day: TWEAK.** The mottled smog clouds run through the text zone. Flatten their contrast in x 15-85% and keep the heavier billows for the edges and horizon. The heavier steam plumes are good.
- **backdrop-overcast-night: KEEP.** Moody, readable, and the lamps carry it. Reduce the cloud mottling in the centre slightly.
- **backdrop-snow-day: TWEAK.** The white plumes and snowy roofs are charming. Snow sitting on the gauge glass faces looks wrong, so put frost on the glass instead. Light snow on the airship envelope's top would sell it.
- **backdrop-phone-day: KEEP.** Good composition, and the horizontal pipe along the bottom anchors it. The airship is small but reads.
- **backdrop-phone-night: TWEAK.** Only the left lamp is lit. Add a matching amber glow on the right pipe so the bottom isn't lopsided.
- **backdrop-phone-overcast-day: KEEP.** Consistent with the desktop version, and the centre is calm.
- **topbar-band-day: TWEAK.** Correct but generic plumbing. Make every third collar a flanged joint with a tiny valve wheel or a small gauge so the repeat has rhythm. As it stands it reads as a towel rail.
- **topbar-band-night: TWEAK.** Same as day. The amber highlight is too uniform; give it gentle lamp-glow hot spots at the joints.
- **wx-gears: REDO as weather.** The gears are well rendered, but gears drifting through the sky make no sense. Turn them into a "flourish" asset if one is wanted, and replace the weather sheet (see section 4).
- **avatar-char: KEEP.** A handsome clockwork medallion.
- **avatar-user: KEEP.** The aviator goggles are perfect: they are the adventurer's badge. This is the most on-theme piece in the set.

### 3. Missing or wrong

- **No sky-adventure infrastructure.** There is no mooring mast or sky-dock gantry on the skyline and no tethered ship. A single docking tower at the left or right edge of the horizon would change the whole mood.
- **No depth in the sky.** It needs cumulus or cloud-sea layers, sunlit cloud edges, distant birds or a third far-off ship, and a sun glow behind the haze on the day version.
- **Too many gears.** The corners are about 70% gears. Swap some for a pressure-boiler dome, a riveted ironwork railing, a lamp post or a brass telescope on a tripod pointing skyward. That telescope is the adventure motif.
- **Missing details:** pennants or a trailing guy-rope on the airship; the soft ochre/teal of Victorian travel-poster colour; cast-iron filigree.
- **Missing variants:** the snow-night backdrop and the remaining phone weather variants.

### 4. Weather

- **Day clear:** drifting soot motes and tiny curls of steam. Soft grey-white wisps, semi-transparent, slow and slightly upward.
- **Night clear:** rising chimney embers (amber specks with a faint glow) and the occasional moth near the lamps.
- **Overcast/rain:** the app draws the rain, so add a steam/smog particle: large, soft, low-opacity brown-grey haze puffs drifting sideways. At night, add rare drifting sparks.
- **Snow:** the app draws the snow, so add soot-flecked ash flakes mixed in, plus white steam puffs.
- **Optional whimsy:** paper flyers or tickets fluttering (blank, no text), or tiny distant airship silhouettes as a very rare, very slow particle. Either gives the "sky full of traffic" feel.

### 5. Top 3 redesign moves

1. **Give the sky depth and a destination.** Add a distant mooring mast or sky-dock at one edge of the horizon and soft layered cloud banks below y 70%. Keep the centre calm, but make it feel like open air, not paper.
2. **Replace the wx-gears weather with atmosphere particles:** steam wisps and soot by day, embers by night, haze puffs in smog. Gears are not weather.
3. **Cut the gear count in the corners by about half** and bring in adventure props: a telescope, a lamp post, a railing, a boiler. Then give the topbar pipe rhythmic valve and gauge joints so the trim feels engineered, not extruded.
