## Nautical (Saltwind Harbor): judge report

Note: there are **no wx-*.png weather sheets** in `/tmp/claude-1000/-home-kolache-Documents-GitHub-Marinara-Engine--claude-worktrees-objective-lamport-25df1d/4a483336-2a08-4dcb-b8f9-7aadb2b4ab0d/scratchpad/judge/nautical/`, and none were requested in the prompts. There is also no phone overcast-night or phone snow backdrop, and no snow-night backdrop. I judged the 12 images that are there.

### 1. Verdict: 5/10
Ahoy. This is a clean, usable *lighthouse postcard*, but it isn't yet Saltwind Harbor. The centres are calm and the red-and-white tower is cheerful. The style, though, is smooth vector-meets-digital-matte, not crisp gouache: no visible brushwork, no chalky flat colour, no folk-art warmth. The scene has no harbour and no cottage in it. It shows open sea, a single post and a lighthouse, so it looks more like a stock "coastal" wallpaper than somewhere cosy to live. The storm variants turn photoreal and moody, so the set doesn't hold together as one illustration across weathers. The avatars are the most charming pieces. The rope trim is wrong in colour and alignment.

### 2. Per image
- backdrop-day: TWEAK. Calm centre and good layout, but it reads as flat digital vector. Re-render with visible gouache texture (paper grain, opaque brush strokes, slightly chalky sky) and push the navy and signal red so the colour isn't generic sky-blue.
- backdrop-night: TWEAK. The beam runs into the calm zone and sweeps across the top 30% of the sky toward the centre. Shorten it to the top 12% near the right edge. Otherwise the navy is lovely and the lit sailboat lantern is a nice touch.
- backdrop-overcast-day: REDO. The style jumps to near-photoreal, and the dramatic breaking spray and streaky clouds clash with the gouache day image. Keep the composition but paint it in the same flat gouache, with stylised curling folk-art waves.
- backdrop-overcast-night: REDO. The heavy cloud mass covers the top 25% and spills inward, and the beam crosses toward the centre. The sailboat survived even though storm variants are meant to drop it, and the style is again more realistic than the rest. Repaint in gouache, remove the boat, and keep the beam short.
- backdrop-snow-day: TWEAK. Snow caps on the rope, post and rocks are charming. Remove the gulls, which the brief dropped elsewhere and which look odd in a winter scene. Add some cold warmth to the sky, such as a pale peach low on the horizon, because the flat grey-white feels lifeless.
- backdrop-phone-day: KEEP, with the same gouache texture pass as the desktop day image. The calm band is excellent.
- backdrop-phone-night: KEEP. The beam is short and correct. This is the best-behaved night image.
- backdrop-phone-overcast-day: TWEAK. Same realism drift as the desktop storm. Gouache it, and soften the streaky clouds in the upper half.
- avatar-char: KEEP. A lovely brass porthole with rivets and a sail. Very on theme.
- avatar-user: TWEAK. It reuses the porthole ring, so the two avatars are hard to tell apart at small sizes. Give it a thinner rope-twist rim or a navy enamel medallion edge, as the prompt asked, and a slightly warmer, folk-art compass rose.
- topbar-band-day: REDO. The rope is grey-silver like steel cable, not hemp (#c9a46a). It is also only about 15% of the strip height, not 30%, and the twist barely reads. Repaint it as a fat tan hemp rope with clear diagonal lays.
- topbar-band-night: REDO. It sits in a different place from the day strip: the rope is at rows 32-38 of 47 with transparency beneath it, while the day rope sits flush at the bottom. Swapping day and night would make the trim jump. Match the day position exactly.

### 3. Missing or wrong
- **Harbour and cottage life are absent.** A fan expects whitewashed cottages with navy shutters, bunting or nautical signal pennants (plain colour blocks, no letters), lobster pots, buoys, a stacked crate, a striped awning and a mooring cleat. Even one cottage roof and a string of pennants on the left edge would turn this sea view into a harbour.
- **Missing motifs:** knots (a reef knot, a monkey's fist or a coiled Flemish flake on the post), anchors, a ship's wheel, life rings in red and white, and Breton stripes anywhere.
- **Material:** brass appears only in the avatars. The backdrops and trim have none.
- **Style:** the "crisp gouache" never really appears. The day images are digital-smooth and the storms are photoreal. That clash is the biggest problem for the set's consistency.
- **Missing sets:** phone overcast-night, phone snow, and desktop snow-night (a snowy night lighthouse with a warm lamp would be the cosiest image in the set).

### 4. Weather (none delivered yet)
- **Day fair:** small white gull silhouettes and drifting gouache clouds, plus a few sea-foam flecks or tiny white sails.
- **Night fair:** soft four-point stars and the occasional distant lantern glint, warm gold.
- **Rain (day and night):** short slanted strokes in slate blue, with a few salt-spray droplets in a lighter teal.
- **Storm:** windblown spume blobs and torn white foam ribbons blowing sideways.
- **Snow:** chunky gouache flakes with a slight hand-cut paper feel, white by day and pale blue by night.
- **Fog (a good fit for a harbour):** soft low sea-mist wisps drifting horizontally, pale grey by day and moonlit blue by night.
- **Autumn or wind:** small signal pennant scraps or tumbling rope ends would be a playful option.

### 5. Top 3 redesign moves
1. **Repaint all backdrops in one true gouache style** (opaque matte strokes, paper grain, flat shapes, stylised curling waves), and in particular pull the three storm images back from photorealism.
2. **Make it a harbour, not open sea.** Put a whitewashed cottage with navy shutters, a string of plain signal pennants, a red-and-white life ring and a lobster pot along the left edge, and swap the plain post for a bollard with a proper knot or Flemish coil.
3. **Fix the trim and supply weather.** Use tan hemp rope at 30% height in the same position for day and night, ideally with a small brass grommet or knot repeat. Deliver the wx sheets (gulls, stars, spray and sea-fog for the theme, plus the standard rain and snow) and the missing phone and night variants.
