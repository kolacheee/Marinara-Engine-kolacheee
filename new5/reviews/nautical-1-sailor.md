## Nautical (Saltwind Harbor) judging report

All 12 nautical previews reviewed. **No `wx-*.png` weather sheets exist in the folder**, and phone overcast-night, phone snow and desktop snow-night are also missing.

**1. Verdict: 6/10**
As a lighthouse postcard it's tidy, and the calm centres mostly work. But it isn't a *harbour*, and it isn't really *gouache*. The sky is smooth digital gradient with no visible brush or paper texture, so the pictures read as stock "lighthouse wallpaper". Brass, one of the four named colours, never appears in a single backdrop. Navy, white and red are there. The day, storm and snow variants hold together well as one set, because the lighthouse, rocks and post stay locked in place. A sailor would also call out a few seamanship errors, listed below.

**2. Per image**
- backdrop-day: TWEAK. Clean and the centre is calm. Add real brush texture. Give it harbour scale: a breakwater or a can buoy near the rocks. The post is a dock pile with turns of rope round it, which is fine.
- backdrop-night: TWEAK. The beam is too fat and runs from y≈5% to 27%, crossing into the calm zone. It also stops dead mid-sky. Make it a thin, fading fan inside the top 15%. The boat's glow sits inside the sail like a cabin lamp; it should be a small white masthead or stern light. Otherwise good.
- backdrop-overcast-day: KEEP. The spray breaking on the rocks is the best thing in the set. The sky is soft enough for text.
- backdrop-overcast-night: TWEAK. Remove the sailboat. No one has sails up in a gale at night, and the storm-day version already removed it. The beam lighting the cloud undersides is lovely; keep it.
- backdrop-snow-day: TWEAK. The rope is painted solid white, as if it had turned to snow. Show hemp with snow only on top. The puffy white summer clouds on a white winter sky look odd; remove them or make them flat grey. The lamp lit in daylight is acceptable on a grey day.
- backdrop-phone-day: TWEAK. The lighthouse reaches up to about 60% of the height, so it enters the 13–86% calm band at x 82–100%. Shrink it and drop it lower, keeping the lantern below about 80%.
- backdrop-phone-night: REDO. The beam fires left at about 64% height, right through the text area. Either lower the whole lighthouse or show only a lit lantern with no beam.
- backdrop-phone-overcast-day: TWEAK. Same lighthouse-height problem as phone-day. The storm treatment is good.
- topbar-band-day: REDO. It's a thin grey-silver strand, about 10% of the strip height, that reads as steel wire, not hemp. It needs to be fat three-strand laid rope at about 30% of the height in #c9a46a, with clear diagonal lay.
- topbar-band-night: REDO. It's brown but still a thin wire, and it floats about 70% of the way down with transparency under it instead of sitting on the bottom edge. Match a corrected day band.
- avatar-char: TWEAK. The porthole idea is good, but it has 8 bolts (6 were asked for) and the brass is cartoon yellow-gold. A real porthole has a hinge lug and two or three wing-nut "dogs". Push the brass toward warm, worn #c9a24a with some verdigris in the crevices.
- avatar-user: TWEAK. The red and white points are nearly the same length, so it reads as a sailor-tattoo "nautical star", not a compass rose. Make the four cardinal points clearly longer than the four in between. Mark north with a fleur-de-lis tip (that's not a letter), and engrave plain degree ticks round the rim. Swap the bolted porthole rim for a smooth brass bezel so the two avatars don't look like twins.

**3. Missing or wrong**
- **No harbour life.** There are no moored boats, bollards or cleats, quay stonework, buoys (red can or green cone), fish crates or lobster pots. The brief's own name says "Harbor".
- **Brass is absent** from every backdrop. Candidates: a ship's bell on the post, a brass lantern hanging from a bracket, or a brass-capped bollard.
- **Signal flags:** the prompt banned "flags with symbols", but plain geometric code-flag bunting (stripes, crosses, squares, no letters) is the most "Saltwind" motif there is. A string of it at a top corner would be safe and very on-theme.
- **Rope rendering:** the rope coil is believable, but nowhere is there proper rope-work such as a cleat hitch or bowline.
- **Light realism:** a lighthouse beam is a narrow cone, not a comet tail. The night boat's lights are wrong as noted above.
- **Clash:** fluffy cumulus clouds pasted into both the night and snow skies.

**4. Weather (none delivered; this is what it should have)**
- **Day, fair:** small gulls gliding and banking in silhouette, plus little white foam flecks and spindrift that drift low.
- **Night, fair:** slow, low wisps of sea mist, and a faint sweeping lighthouse beam as an overlay sprite on a slow rotation, kept to the top edge.
- **Rain (day and night):** rain driven nearly sideways with salt-spray droplet clusters. Spray at night is grey-silver, lit warm near the beam.
- **Snow:** wet, heavy flakes on a diagonal wind, not drifting fluff.
- **Fog (strongly recommended as an extra variant):** rolling sea-fog banks, the quintessential harbour weather. They'd pair with a dimmed lighthouse halo.

**5. Top 3 redesign moves**
1. **Make it a harbour and add brass.** Put a quay, bollard, buoy and code-flag bunting along the edges, and add at least one brass object per backdrop. This is what turns it from a generic lighthouse into Saltwind Harbor.
2. **Fix readability on phone and at night.** Lower or shrink the phone lighthouse so it stays out of the 13–86% band. Keep every beam inside the top 15% (desktop) or drop it (phone). Remove the sailboat from storms.
3. **Redo the rope trim and deliver the weather sheets.** Draw a fat three-strand hemp rope on the bottom edge for both day and night. Ship `wx` sheets for gulls, spray, sea mist and wind-driven squall, and ideally a fog variant. Add real gouache brush and paper texture throughout.
