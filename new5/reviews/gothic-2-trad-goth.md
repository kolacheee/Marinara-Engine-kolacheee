## Gothic (Nightshade Parlour) judging report

I looked at all 17 previews and checked the trim strip's pixel rows directly, since it is too thin to judge by eye.

**1. Verdict: 6.5/10.** The night set is tasteful and very usable: the plum-black field is calm, the full moon sits up in the corner, and the lantern on the gate is a lovely touch. But as someone from the 1980s scene I'd call it Valentine's-card gothic, not crypt gothic. It has red roses, a pretty fence and cute bats, but nothing that says ruins, mourning or the grave: no stone, no arches, no ivy, no velvet, no silver candelabra. It looks like a florist's window after dark. The day/night pairing does work. Every scene keeps the same composition and the same palette logic (pastel bloom by day, oxblood and black roses by night), so it reads as one theme. The weakest link is the trim strip, which is broken.

**2. Per image**
- backdrop-night: TWEAK. The centre is well judged. The edges need more gothic mass: a ruined lancet arch or broken stone pillar behind each rose column, and ivy on the fence. The bats are drawn beside the moon, not against it, so move one bat over the moon's disc.
- backdrop-overcast-night: KEEP. Best image in the set. The lantern pool on the wet flagstones is exactly right. Optionally add faint wet sheen on the ironwork.
- backdrop-phone-night: TWEAK. Match the desktop fix by putting a ruined stone arch fragment or a weathered headstone silhouette behind the rose column. The right half of the bottom is a bare fence with nothing growing on it, so add a few black-rose sprigs at the bottom right.
- backdrop-day: KEEP. A convincing pastel goth version, and the centre is clean.
- backdrop-overcast-day: KEEP. The wet petals and glossy iron read well. The sky could be a touch greyer to separate it from the snow scene.
- backdrop-snow-day: KEEP. Charming, and there is no falling snow painted in.
- backdrop-phone-day: KEEP.
- backdrop-phone-overcast-day: KEEP.
- topbar-band-day: REDO. There is no lace. It is a hairline wobble about 6 px tall in the middle of the 47 px preview, not a scalloped lace edge filling the bottom 45%. The pink ribbon, bat-wing motifs and rose motifs are all missing. Regenerate with heavy, clearly scalloped black lace anchored to the bottom edge.
- topbar-band-night: REDO. Same failure, and there is no crimson velvet ribbon or silver thread. Redo it as a recolour of the fixed day strip.
- wx-petals: KEEP.
- wx-petals-night: KEEP. Velvety and correct. The black-red petal (third) may vanish against the night sky, so give it a faint crimson rim highlight.
- wx-bats: TWEAK. These are kawaii clip-art bats with big eyes, which suits the day theme but undercuts the night. Make a night set of true silhouettes: no eyes, ragged membrane wings, with a thin silver rim.
- avatar-char: KEEP. A lovely cameo.
- avatar-char-night: KEEP. Onyx, ivory and antique silver is exactly the Victorian mourning-jewellery look.
- avatar-user: KEEP.
- avatar-user-night: KEEP. To make it more authentically mourning jewellery, add a tiny jet bead or a seed-pearl border.

**3. Missing or wrong**
- **Motifs missing:** gothic architecture (pointed arches, tracery, a ruined chapel wall), weeping ivy, cobwebs on the finials, candelabra or tapers, moths, ravens, mourning veil or crape, jet beads, and lilies or nightshade next to the roses. Crosses and headstones are on the prohibited list, so lean on arches, urns and ivy instead.
- **Materials:** black velvet and tarnished silver appear only in the avatars. The backdrops use no fabric or stone textures, just sky and iron.
- **Palette:** the night palette is right. Add a cold silver-blue moonlight rim on the stone and iron so it isn't all warm plum.
- **What clashes:** the cute bats in night scenes, and the scattered petals on the ground. They read as confetti, so cut them to two or three.
- **Missing variants:** there is no snow-night backdrop, no phone overcast-night and no phone snow. A snowy moonlit night (frosted black roses, lantern glow on snow) would be the crown jewel of a goth set.

**4. Weather**
- **Day, clear:** pastel petals (as made), plus the occasional cute bat.
- **Day, rain:** the app draws the rain itself. Add a sprite of a few drifting wet pastel petals.
- **Night, clear:** silhouette bats (redrawn as above), a few crimson and black petals, and a new sheet of 2 or 3 pale grey moths with a faint silver dust trail.
- **Night, storm:** the app's rain, plus a rare distant lightning flash that briefly silvers the cloud edges (an effect, not a sprite). There should be no bats here, which matches the backdrop.
- **Snow, day and night:** the app's snow. Optionally a sprite of slow-falling black petals for night.
- **Night ambient extras:** a thin low ground fog creeping along the fence (a transparent wisp sprite), and candle-ember motes near the bottom.

**5. Top 3 redesign moves**
1. **Redo the lace trim properly.** It is currently unusable: there is no lace, ribbon or motifs. Make visible scalloped black lace with a ribbon, then recolour it for night.
2. **Give the night backdrops a gothic structure at the edges.** Put a ruined lancet arch or a mossy stone pillar with ivy behind each rose column, add cobwebs on two finials, and silver moonlight edges on the stone. The centre stays exactly as it is. This turns the florist window into a moonlit ruin.
3. **Split the bats into day (cute) and night (true silhouettes, no eyes), add a moth sprite, and make the missing variants.** The variants are snow-night, phone overcast-night and phone snow.
