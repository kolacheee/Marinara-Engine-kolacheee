## Crepuscular (slug pancake), batch 3: an ordinary moon in the theme's print style, and the phone skies cleaned
## Style for every image: as batch 1 and 2 (pancake/bg-day.png): a painted illustration in muted gouache over fine etched ink linework, like a hand-tinted plate from a 19th-century astronomy book (Nasmyth and Carpenter's lunar plates); fine stipple and engraved hatching, soft paper grain; never photographic, never airbrushed, never vector-clean; no text, letters, numbers or signatures anywhere.
## The owner's rule for this batch: the moon is JUST A NORMAL MOON. No face, no eyes, no eye glints, no mouth, no freckles arranged like a face, no cat head, no ears, no pancake, no eclipse. Avoid any two dark spots side by side that could read as eyes: the seas must sit in their real places on the real near side of the moon, and nothing else.
