# Round 17 — panel 1 art direction

Merged from all five reviews against BRIEF-17. R means final refinement. Reassign E's signs to A, streets to C, and life details to R.

**The three things the panel agreed matter most**

- Convincing volumes: deep roofs, substantial walls, corners and deliberate yards, matching the town houses.
- Recognisable destinations: civic anchor, gate, shops and market, connected by clear routes.
- Readability at 1x: quiet paving, coherent materials and fewer repeated building arrangements.

**Changes, highest priority first**

1. **[C] Approve a combined pilot before expanding A.** Assemble an 8×8-cell corner block with facade, roof, side entrance, court, adjoining junction and person; reserve civic/gate positions.
   Draw separate ground footprints, painted bounds, entrance anchors and permitted overhangs.

2. **[A] Give the facade substance.** Draw 96×48 ground/upper modules with a stone plinth top, substantial posts, recessed windows, projecting sills and a shaded 6–8 px jetty.
   Match `fantasy-house-2` at 1x/4x without decoration; size windows by bay, not a blanket 25% increase.

3. **[B] Build roof volumes.** Attach 96/144 px-deep caps with broad tile clusters, ridge, thick eave, hip and valley joins to the pilot.
   Join unequal heights with plain party walls, coping and flashing; use directional fills and gutters, not universal aligned eaves. Dormers stay optional.

4. **[C] Draw deeper properties.** Use 1–2-cell fronts on 5–7-cell-deep plots; allow merged 3-cell fronts. Fit a house, fenced yard and low rear shed.
   Add separately lit east/west returns, side doors and a 48 px court passage. Preserve deliberate yards; reject accidental roof holes.

5. **[C] Clarify circulation.** Keep 96 px clear from gate through square and 48 px on side routes after props. Draw quiet 6–12 px cobbles, gutters and junctions.
   Tie setbacks to properties; drop compulsory wedges/jogs and defer 24 px lanes until clearance is proven.

6. **[D] Establish one skyline leader.** Draw a north-edge church and enclosed yard, with a lower arcaded guildhall opposite.
   Treat brief dimensions as footprints; allow roughly 432 px church art height, tower 48 px above neighbouring roofs. Lower flanking houses; fountains and market halls stay low.

7. **[D] Show the way out.** Draw a 4-cell-wide gatehouse, 96 px opening, road beyond, connected wall-walk/stair and one interval tower; keep the inner 48 px service route.
   Choose the level/player/technical reviewers' wide gate over the historian's choke point to preserve circulation.

8. **[A] Clarify doors and trades.** Usable doors get roughly 20×30 px openings, lit steps, lanterns and clear 48×48 approaches; service doors stay shuttered.
   Resolve disputed arch readability with paving/daylight continuing through. Identify bakery, inn and apothecary through large goods shapes and 24 px symbol signs.

9. **[D] Make a usable market.** Draw six 48×48 stalls with 36–44 px canopies above people's heads, counters and distinct goods; offset a 96×96 fountain.
   Resolve rows versus clusters with two staggered groups of three, a 96 px aisle, 48 px end access and clear gate route.

10. **[A] Calm materials and seams.** Keep warm plaster, timber and stone bases coherent per building; use a dominant muted roof family with occasional repairs.
    Preserve 48 px registration, using compatible joins/adapters instead of repeated floor belts. Keep 1 px silhouettes, quieter lit edges and no doubled shared outlines.

11. **[B] Ground buildings with shadows.** Draw 3–6 px eave/jetty shade and height-responsive south/east ground shadows; retain paving texture and avoid doubled dark seams.
    Allow controlled projections beyond plot edges while preserving routes. Mask shadows to receiving surfaces; sunlit streets may pass.

12. **[R] Add purposeful life and variety.** Place washing/wood by rear doors, vendors behind counters, customers in front and a jug-filler at the fountain; retain the bench pocket.
    Vary building heights, door/bay layouts and roof accents; add one courtyard surprise, not random clutter.

13. **[R] Draw the acceptance sheet.** Compare town references at 1x/3x and greyscale; show compatible unlike seams, both tall/low joins and a marked player behind roofs, under arches and beside stalls, with needed cutaways.
    Reject hidden doors/actors, blocked routes and repeated silhouettes; four review rounds cannot waive failures.

Cap this round at a 40–60-piece pilot in one material family. Defer 400–450 exports, twelve block plans, twenty-seed testing, extra civic attachments and the full district/animal catalogue. Pathfinding, collision, click routing and live fading belong to later engine work; draw clearance/cutaway proofs here.
