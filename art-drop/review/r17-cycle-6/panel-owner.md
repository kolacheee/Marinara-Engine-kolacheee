# Owner review — round 17, cycle 6

**Ship decision: no.** This is a more useful direction than today's rejected fantasy town, but it still looks like a showcase of city assets rather than a finished place to walk around. Round 15 had stronger continuous street frontage; round 17 improves depth and landmarks while leaving too much of the district as exposed paving. “Better than definitely a no” is not the shipping bar.

Reviewed the full final at 1× and 2×, the comparison sheet, both source comparison renders, and all `fantasy-house-*` / `fantasy-hall-*` references. Made and inspected Pillow nearest-neighbour 3× crops of the square `(720,624)–(1200,1104)`, west side street `(288,336)–(720,768)`, gate `(1104,1008)–(1440,1440)`, and north wall `(432,48)–(912,288)`. Crops stayed in memory. No `critic-r17R*` squint file exists in this checkout; inspected `r17-city-final-squint.png` and the C5 square/gate squints instead. Coordinates below refer to the 1920×1440 final, from its upper-left corner. One cell is 48 px.

## 1. What works

- Street frontages are paved, without lawns. Roof planes, shadows, substantial stalls, and recognizable civic buildings are improvements over the supplied comparisons.
- The cathedral and twin gate towers are identifiable at 1×. Several trade pictograms can be named immediately.

## 2. Blocking problems

1. **The district is not dense enough, and its streets are still gaps between exhibits.** Most obvious in the northwest properties `(144–672,288–720)`, northeast properties `(1392–1776,288–720)`, and scattered southern blocks `(144–1776,1104–1344)`. Narrow house stacks and pairs of freestanding side-view houses leave long paved slots inside supposed blocks. The broad northern apron reinforces the display-board effect. There are individual offsets, but no persuasive network of enclosed streets, roof-filled blocks, and concealed back courts. Today's town's detached-lot problem has partly survived the removal of grass.

2. **The square is marked by landmarks more successfully than it is framed by them.** Around `(480–1200,624–1200)`, the guildhall's occupied body is squeezed beneath the surrounding residential roofline; its clock supplies most of its authority. The cathedral's main steps face south, away from the square on its east. The southern market hall presents its roof toward the square and its open arcade toward the bottom of the picture. The pale paving and neatly arranged objects do most of the work of defining the civic space. I want a square whose buildings address it, not a pale patch beside important sprites.

3. **The architectural language still splits between solid houses and facade strips.** Compare the west side-street houses around `(336–576,288–672)` with the stack at `(576–672,336–672)`, then the thin units beside the guildhall and the four narrow southern roofs at `(528–720,1104–1200)`. The references have coherent roof, wall, entrance, and foundation masses. Several new stacks look like independent window panels piled under an extremely narrow roof. Alternating ornate red panels, blank cream panels, and unrelated window shapes makes their construction harder to believe. Adding a deep roof has not solved the cardboard below it.

4. **The ground treatment does not give a clean movement hierarchy.** Across the central east–west street, the square, and the gate approach `(1056–1440,1008–1440)`, broad pale bands, cobbles, and rectangular paving patches compete. Property-sized colour boundaries and blunt shadow ends expose the assembly grid. In greyscale the gate remains recognizable, but the main route is less decisive than the bright horizontal paving bands. A player should read the route into the square before reading the cobble texture. This is a visual finding; the render does not establish collision behaviour.

5. **Repetition and inconsistent material scale remain conspicuous.** The north wall `(0–1920,96–240)` alternates visibly different masonry scales at hard vertical joins. Its recurring damage patches and near-identical tower silhouettes dominate a large part of the view. Paired side houses in both northern quarters and the southern roof row repeat the same silhouettes too openly. The northern towers do cycle through three artwork variants, so this is not a claimed two-in-five breach. The result still looks copied. Texture swaps are insufficient variation at gameplay scale.

6. **Shop identification is louder than interaction clarity.** The smith/cobbler fronts `(144–432,768–1008)`, apothecary near `(1104,552)`, and eastern trades `(1296–1776,816–1056)` use signs approaching the size of an entire storey, while their actual thresholds are much less consistent. Some ground-floor openings are dark rectangles; others have detailed steps or clutter. I can recognize a giant scissors emblem without being equally certain where I enter. The icons should support readable shops, not carry the whole frontage.

## 3. Ranked suggestions

1. **Rebuild the block footprints first.** Start with the two northern residential groups. Connect their freestanding side houses to 96- or 144-px-deep rear wings using `roof-fill`, `roof-valley`, and matching side/rear walls. Keep a 48-px internal wall lane, 96-px main streets, 48-px side streets, and selected 24-px service lanes. Any remaining internal opening should be an enclosed court with a visible entrance, not street paving leaking through the block. Put a 48-px frontage jog or junction within each 384-px street run. Use buildings and enclosed service courts to occupy surplus space; do not introduce frontage lawns.

2. **Make the square's buildings face and contain it.** Bring the 192-px-wide guildhall frontage south to base row `y=720`; lower its immediately flanking houses by one 48-px storey. Give the cathedral a clearly visible 96×48-px entrance/steps piece on its east flank, approximately `(672,912)–(768,960)`, addressing the square. Redraw the 192×144-px southern market hall with an arcade readable from the square side. Keep the fountain's 96×96-px footprint as the main open-space anchor and retain six 48×48-px stalls with a clear 48-px aisle. Make the surrounding frontages establish the boundary before adjusting paving decoration.

3. **Approve one complete house before rebuilding the remaining stacks.** Use `fantasy-house-2` beside a 96-px-wide merchant house at 1×. Build that house from 96×48-px storeys, a 96- or 144-px-deep roof, a visible side return at an exposed end, and one continuous foundation. Enlarge the small window openings roughly 25%, retain one timber structure through the floors, and limit the block to two wall colours and one roof family. Replace some adjacent 48-px slivers with these wider houses; reserve narrow units for occasional infill.

4. **Draw the gate route as one connected 96-px strip.** Reserve the approach around `x=1200–1296`, with a westward turn through `y=1056–1152` toward the square. Continue its kerbs behind the gatehouse where the building occludes it. Use 48×48-px straight, corner, and junction paving pieces with a consistent gutter; remove unrelated pale rectangles along that route. Keep side streets darker and less contrasted. Replace rectangular shadow mats with south/east silhouette shadows, including visible contact shadow at foundations.

5. **Unify stone scale and vary structures.** Redraw the north wall as compatible 96×144-px face sections with one stone-course scale and matching end pixels. Make three genuinely different 96×144-px upper tower treatments—roofed watch post, open battlement, and banner/bell variant—while retaining compatible lower shafts. Give the repeated side houses alternative 96×144-px roof/upper-wall assemblies with a changed ridge or dormer silhouette. Enforce the two-in-five rule and also inspect the sequence at 1× for obvious reskins.

6. **Standardize useful thresholds, then reduce sign dominance.** Make a 48×48-px enterable-door module containing a recessed opening, lit step, and a small lantern; give blind openings a visibly different shuttered treatment. Apply it consistently to the named shops. Redraw their signs around 24×32 px, carried by an 8–12-px projecting bracket near the ground/first-floor boundary, and keep each symbol legible at 1×. Add one 48×48-px trade display beside each key entrance, outside the clear walking lane.
