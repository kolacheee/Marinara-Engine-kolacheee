**4) Where I disagree with BRIEF-17**

- **“Taller than any 5-storey stack” should not apply to every landmark.** The cathedral spire and main gate can dominate the skyline. A market hall or fountain should earn its importance through position, space around it and activity. Making all civic pieces gigantic will make ordinary life feel miniature.
- **“A block uses at most 2 wall colours and 1 roof colour” is too rigid.** Keep a dominant family, but allow one repaired roof section, an older stone house or a modest material exception. E's red roof bands already show how easily consistency becomes sameness. I am not asking for round 15's bright patchwork back.
- **“Hanging signs twice the round 15 size” measures the wrong thing.** Recognition matters alongside a visible entrance and believable mounting. The E signs demonstrate that passing recognition alone can damage the street's scale.
- **Six to eight stalls “in rows” and a compulsory width change every eight cells should be options, not recipes.** A market needs customers' routes and service space; a bend should reveal a destination or follow a building. Repeating the same jog and two stall strips across districts will produce another kind of grid.
- **“Overlays never cross a lot edge” needs an exception for shared street life.** The central washing line is one of E block's best invitations to look closer. Let a supported line, banner or vine connect facing buildings where the walking route remains clear.

I want a crowded city of recognisable homes, working corners and tempting side passages; this round still gives me too many repeated buildings standing around waiting to become a place.
