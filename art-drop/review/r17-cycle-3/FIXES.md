# Round 17 cycle 3 fix list (from cycles 1-2, run with Claude reviewers while Codex was out)

Cycle 2 panel scores: tech 8, RPG 7, pixel 7, historian 6, owner 6. Goal: every reviewer 8 or more.

## Layout (code)
1. Break the grid: make 2-3 blocks trapezoids or L-shapes, bend one street, cut 1-2 narrow alleys through blocks, vary block widths, and add yards and setbacks.
2. Pick one roof colour per building. Change ridge direction or height against the neighbour, so rows don't read as stripes of colour slabs.
3. Mix 2-5 storeys within a block, with steps of 1-2.
4. Make the inn a landmark: move it onto the gate road or the square's edge, with a bigger frontage, a courtyard and a stable.
5. Wall: space the towers unevenly, add a corner bastion, and add a ditch or river on one side.
6. Remove the narrow brown strip roof next to the guildhall (around x 1056-1104, y 290-550). Give the top-right block's flat end slab a hip.

## Drawing (Codex)
1. A street cobble set with random stones, dirt, puddles, gutters and kerbs, plus paving that blends into the square and the gate lane with no rectangles. Feather the edges where grass meets paving or wall.
2. 4-5 more shopfront variants.
3. Roof chimneys, dormers, ridge caps, moss and patch variants, and hip versions of the deep slab roofs.
4. Street furniture: lamps, planters, tub trees, barrels, carts and washing lines.
