# Owner review — cycle 5

**Do not ship.** This is still a modular-art demonstration, not a convincing district I would want to walk around in my game. More objects and more rendering have not solved the composition.

Reviewed the full final at 1× and 2×, the comparison sheet, round 15, today's fantasy panel, the house/hall references, and `BRIEF-17.md`. Pillow nearest-neighbour 3× crops were made and viewed in memory: square `(720,720)–(1104,1056)`, east side street `(1344,480)–(1632,864)`, gate/wall junction `(1152,1056)–(1392,1440)`, and northwest roofs `(144,288)–(432,624)`. Coordinates below refer to the 1920×1440 final, origin at top left. No `critic-r17R*squint*` file exists in this checkout; the reviewed greyscale is the newer `r17-city-final-squint.png`.

## 1. What works

Street frontages have no lawns. The church, clock hall and gate have recognisable silhouettes; the gate approach is visible. Stalls are finally large enough to read, and cast shadows give the better buildings weight. Those are useful corrections, not shipping clearance.

## 2. Blocking problems

1. **The roof system still does not describe believable buildings.** Most obvious at the northwest block, `(144,288)–(432,720)`, and repeated across the north row and southern houses. Long, flat-topped rectangles of tiny horizontal tiles abruptly become differently textured hip/gable pieces. At `(144,384)` and `(336,384)` the change in roof drawing is a visible construction joint. Some roofs look like vertically stretched panels. Their tiny tile texture and straight rectangular backs disagree with the large, clearly pitched planes of the church, market hall and reference houses. The nearby whole building at approximately `(576,336)` makes the mismatch impossible to excuse. This fails the brief's basic test: these do not all look like neighbours drawn for the same game.

2. **It is not dense enough in the way a city needs to be dense.** The northeast spaces around `(1344,624)–(1776,768)`, the west passages around `(288,480)–(480,768)`, and the gaps between southern groups remain broad areas of undifferentiated paving. There are more storeys, but the groups still sit separately on a common ground sheet. The long paved notches inside groups resemble missing buildings or public alleys, not enclosed rear courts. There are too few convincing street-facing returns to make the north–south routes feel inhabited. Removing today's lawns was necessary; replacing their spatial emptiness with paving is insufficient. Round 15's continuous frontages create stronger enclosure than this version.

3. **The square contains landmarks more successfully than it is framed by them.** In the centre, roughly `(480,624)–(1200,1104)`, the clock hall supplies a north edge, the church supplies a west flank, and the market hall occupies the open area. The east and south boundaries are weak and fragmented. The church's main door opens south, away from the market activity on its east; the little enclosed court behind it does not solve that relationship. The fountain, cross, stalls and hall look arranged on spare paving. There is no strong, irregular civic room. Round 15 lacked landmarks, but its square was easier to identify as a bounded place.

4. **The ground and perimeter hierarchy undermine the walkable view.** Around `(1344,576)–(1632,768)` and throughout the southern half, fine rectangular paving borders outline parcels without clearly explaining street, threshold or private court. The same cobble field serves nearly every purpose. The pale gate route at approximately `x1104–1296` is an improvement, but it meets the market without a comparably clear square surface or secondary-route hierarchy. In greyscale, the repeated bright wall towers and huge north wall compete strongly with the civic centre. The church and gate survive; the square as a place does not. This is a visual navigation failure, not a claim that collision or pathfinding has been tested.

5. **The finishing language is still visibly assembled from incompatible scales and repetitions.** Bread at approximately `(752,576)`, medicine at `(1136,672)`, and the boot, scissors and quill signs occupy enormous blank facade panels. They read as interface badges pasted onto buildings. Meanwhile the stalls at `(936,756)–(1080,948)` use the same vendor six times, and repeated window/storey treatments dominate multiple groups. The signs are identifiable, but their scale damages the architecture. The market looks staffed by duplicated placeholders. Exact piece-rule compliance alone would not make this visually varied enough to ship.

## 3. Ranked suggestions

1. **Redraw and prove one complete roof assembly before expanding it.** Use the northwest three-lot group as the test. Make coherent 96×96, 96×144 and 144×144 roof volumes, with matching 48×96/144 end pieces. Give each volume a readable ridge, continuous receding slope, shaded right plane and eave shadow. Replace the texture change at `y384` with a drawn structural transition; remove any line that merely marks the module boundary. Match the reference houses' tile scale and plane shading. Keep 1 px outer outlines, hard pixel edges and top-left light. Judge the completed group beside `fantasy-house-2` at 1× before reusing it.

2. **Rebuild blocks around deliberate routes.** Start with the northeast groups in `(1392,288)–(1776,720)`. Add 48×96 or 96×96 rear wings and actual side-ground/side-storey returns to enclose their courts. Preserve 48 px side streets and 24 px service lanes; reserve 96 px for main streets and selected junctions. Add a 48 px jog before any street width continues for eight cells. Close private courts with 48 px gate/fence pieces and distinct dirt or stone floors. Retain the 48 px clear wall lane. Put greenery only inside enclosed courts or tubs, never as frontage lawn.

3. **Give the square three architectural edges and a deliberate entrance.** Keep the clock hall's front on the north rim. Move the 192×144 market hall to the south rim, around `(768,1008)`, replacing/reworking the roof lots it would overlap. Add an east-facing church portal, approximately 48×72, with a 48×48 landing beside its east wall near `(696,912)`. Keep the gate-side opening 96 px wide. Draw one continuous L-shaped square floor of at least 288×240 px, with a 96×96 corner cutout. Reposition the 96×96 fountain within that floor and place the six 48×48 stalls as two rows with a clear 48 px aisle. The landmark bases must establish the boundary before props are added.

4. **Redraw the ground by use, then rebalance the wall.** Give that square larger, quieter paving; carry a continuous 96 px gate route into it; use smaller cobbles on 48 px side streets and darker surfaces inside private courts. Replace freestanding parcel rectangles with kerbs following actual building bases and routes. Use a 2–3 px gutter and consistent south/east shadows. Reduce the north wall's internal texture contrast so its masonry is subordinate to the square's entrances. At the gate/wall junction around `(1176,1344)–(1368,1440)`, preserve the readable opening and make the wall-walk connections explicit with matching 48 px transition pieces. Recheck the complete scene in greyscale, not just isolated assets.

5. **Integrate shop identity into the architecture.** Redraw trade signs at roughly 24×32 px on short 8–12 px brackets beside the corresponding doors. Keep their simple recognisable symbols. Replace the vacated billboard panels with a recessed window, timber bay or an awning appropriate to that shop. Put a lit step and small lantern at every intended entrance, with a full 48 px clear approach. Verify the trades remain nameable at 1×.

6. **Replace obvious clones after the structural fixes.** Give the six vendors at least three visibly different silhouettes/outfits and alternate their positions behind the counters. Add three structural variants for the repeated 48/96 px facade bays: different window placement, an oriel, and a recessed workshop opening. Vary whole bay designs rather than sprinkling extra scratches over identical shapes. Enforce at most two identical pieces in every sliding run of five, then inspect the rendered run for repetition anyway.

BLOCKING: 5
