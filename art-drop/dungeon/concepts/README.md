# Dungeon concept paintings (2026-11-21)

Whole-level concepts, one image-tool painting each (Codex image tool), with only `art-drop/dungeon/reference/master-interiors-mock.png` attached as the style reference. Nothing from earlier tiles, frames or sprites was composited in. Not post-processed: these are the raw 1448x1086 outputs. AI-generated art.

Shortlist: the best two of three variants per dungeon, judged first on style consistency against the master mock.

- keep-1.png, keep-3.png: ruined keep ending in a dragon's hoard lair
- crypt-1.png, crypt-3.png: crypt with ossuary niches, sarcophagi and a ritual circle
- cave-1.png, cave-3.png: natural rock cave with a lava fissure chamber

Shared prompt:

> Use your image generation tool to create exactly one image, with the attached ref-master.png as the style reference: match its pixel-art finish, rich shading, many colours, material detail, wall construction and figure scale exactly (note the reference is stored at 2x). Finished-game quality, high three-quarter top-down orthographic view (top plane plus front faces), pixel art on a regular 48 px cell grid (grid not drawn). Walls are a thin dark top strip plus a tall one-cell wall face with things hung on it (sconces, banners, shelves, racks); square corners in built parts, curves only in round chambers and caves; doors are real gaps in the wall holding a framed door. Floors vary per room (flags, rubble, rugs, dirt, blood, moss). Props differ per room, no repeated stamps, a contact shadow under every prop. Warm light sources (torches, braziers, fires) with soft pools of light rendered in pixels against cool stone. A party of 4 adventurers and a few foes for scale, at the reference's figure scale. No text, no UI, no grid lines. Landscape, fill the frame.

Subject lines:
- Subject: one complete ruined-keep dungeon level of about 24 by 18 cells ending in a dragon's hoard lair: entry stair, guard room, armoury, a collapsed corridor, a locked door, a secret door, a trapped hallway, and the hoard chamber with a big gold heap.
- Subject: one complete crypt level of about 24 by 18 cells: ossuary niches full of skulls set into the walls, stone sarcophagi, burial halls, and a ritual chamber with a glowing circle on the floor.
- Subject: one complete natural rock cave level of about 24 by 18 cells: irregular rock walls with height, rough cave floors, pools, fungus, a camp, and a large chamber split by a glowing lava fissure.
