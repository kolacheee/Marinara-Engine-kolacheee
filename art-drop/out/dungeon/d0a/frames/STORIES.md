# Hoard keep

The garrison stored its pay behind the treasury piers and kept a fire in the guard room.
The vault keeper dragged the remaining wealth downstairs, leaving broken stores along the passage.
Follow the scattered gold past the found secret door to the amber heap and its bound brazier.

# Breach temple

Priests slept beside the offering room and tended a sandstone sanctuary.
A failed rite split the nave; broken masonry leads back through the approach corridor.
The ember fissure draws the eye across the chamber to the standing seal at its eastern end.

# Sanctum crypt

Keepers of the dead catalogued the tombs in the northern reading room.
Their wards became a violet binding circle, enclosing the old burial dais.
Candle groups lead toward the circle and the bright tablet beside its northern edge.

# Rock cave

Miners worked the passages around the pools and left timber racks by the dig faces.
Fungus spread through the abandoned camp while armed scavengers occupied the open ground.
The party's stair, striped trap and two chests mark a route through the eastern chamber.

# Manor ground

A large household shared the dining hall, kitchen and rooms around the carpeted passage.
Intruders entered while the tables were still laid, leaving rubble in the kitchen.
The garden path leads through the front door toward the locked dining wing and the intruders beyond it.

# Station lab

Researchers kept specimen tanks across the corridor from their office and reference shelves.
A tank leaked during a lockdown, and a hostile guard remains in the bay.
The pale tank rims lead toward the spill and red guard; striped hazards mark the route through the airlock.
