# Round 3: wuxia rice paddies (2026-11-06)

The wuxia map's farmland is the "rice paddies" ground, which still has the old look: a huge wobbly blue grid with fringed, rounded edges that spreads like wallpaper. Redraw it the same way as the round-2 farmland, as square one-cell tiles.

Deliver 32 files to `art-drop/out/`: `paddies-0..7.png`, `paddies@spring-0..7.png`, `paddies@autumn-0..7.png` and `paddies@winter-0..7.png`. Each is 48x48 and opaque, in the same three-quarter overhead pixel style and palette as the approved markers and the round-2 farmland (`out/farmland*.png`, `out/wuxia-farmstead*.png`).

1. Fill the tile edge to edge. Thin earthen bunds (raised dirt walls) divide the plots, and along the tile edge there is only a thin bund. No frames, margins or rounded corners.
2. Bunds meet the tile edge at right angles, never diagonally, so tiles join in any order. The occasional layout can have a terrace look, with plots stepping in rows and darker bund shadows, kept square to the cell.
3. Each tile has 1 to 4 plots. Vary the layouts: one big plot, two halves, a 2x2, three strips. Give 1 or 2 layouts a narrow raised footpath along a bund. Give 1 layout a small wuxia farmhouse: a grey tile roof with upturned eaves, a tiny courtyard and perhaps a water buffalo. It sits fully inside the cell, with nothing cut off.
4. Seasons, with the same 8 layouts in each:
   - spring: flooded plots of reflective blue-grey water, with rows of tiny green seedlings
   - summer (no suffix): lush green rice, a little water showing
   - autumn: golden ripe rice, with 1 or 2 plots harvested to stubble and bundled sheaves
   - winter: drained plots under white snow, dark bunds showing through, nothing blue
5. It must read as paddies, not as the round-2 wheat farmland. Water and bunds are the signature. It must not be a recolour of the farmland tiles.

Leave every other file in `out/` alone.
