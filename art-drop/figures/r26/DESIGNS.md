# Five original field-sprite characters

Left to right in the image. All five share roughly three-head proportions, slim legs, small feet, front three-quarter standing poses and upper-left lighting.

## Tavra Quell — canal-lock keeper

Tavra has deep brown skin and copper-red hair swept into an angular curled crest, with a short low braid. Her petrol-teal work coat divides into two long tails; a cream scarf, brass clasps and a compact brass lock-crank establish her waterside trade. Charcoal trousers and small brown boots keep her lower silhouette slender. Her calm, confident face and hand resting at her belt give this practical worker a quietly heroic bearing.

## Orsen Vell — travelling glassworker

Orsen has warm olive skin and thick midnight-blue waves swept across a carefully shaped brow. An oxblood leather bolero frames his ochre shirt, while a sand-coloured asymmetric apron and a sea-green waist sash create long hanging shapes. He cradles a palm-sized turquoise glass vessel at his waist, with narrow dark trousers and small ankle boots completing the design. His attentive expression suggests someone always checking his work against the light.

## Nivet Sorr — storm-post courier

Nivet has light freckled skin and pale silver hair arranged in three windswept clumps with a short nape tail. A burnt-orange capelet and two hanging scarf ends make her silhouette readable against her fitted navy tunic, cream trousers and small navy boots. A sealed cylindrical message case sits close to her hip; one hand steadies her scarf. Her lively eyes and determined brows convey a traveller accustomed to delivering through rough weather.

## Daska Renn — civic bell tuner

Daska has medium brown skin, voluminous dark plum curls and a pale temple lock. His muted violet waistcoat has staggered tails over ivory sleeves, crossed by a long moss-green shoulder sash. A small silver tuning fork hangs from one hand and a tiny wooden mallet is tucked into his belt; charcoal trousers and little buckle shoes preserve the shared proportions. His composed face and measured stance give a humble civic craft a ceremonial dignity.

## Elluma Vask — travelling stage rigger

Elluma has tawny skin and pale golden hair folded into a high loop ponytail above a broad side fringe. A forest-green jacket, warm grey shirt, rust-red sash with hanging ends and short plum shoulder mantle create a layered but readable silhouette. A neat rope coil rests at her hip above narrow navy trousers and little work boots. One gloved hand on her hip and a wry smile suggest the person who can quietly rescue any performance from a backstage mishap.
