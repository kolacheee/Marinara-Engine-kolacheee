# Round 26: five original characters in ONE image, HD-2D field sprites with a refined finish

1. Invent five ORIGINAL characters with new names and designs (pick five different town or traveller roles; do not use a merchant with a big pack, a scholar with a book, a dancer, a hunter with a bow or a cleric with a sun staff). Stylised and slightly heroic even though they are townsfolk. No existing game's characters, art or names; nothing from earlier work. Write out/DESIGNS.md (a short paragraph each).
2. Make ONE image-tool call that draws all five side by side on a flat #FF00FF background, one shared ground line, same scale, nothing else in the frame. No reference images attached. Target look (describe it in the prompt):
   - About 3 heads tall: big stylised hair with clear clumps, slim legs, small feet; slightly heroic.
   - Bold readable silhouettes with flowing elements that hang: capes, scarves, coat tails, sashes.
   - Front three-quarter turn (slightly turned), not a flat mugshot front.
   - A refined finish: refined 4-tone cel shading with a clear light from the upper left, clean pixel clusters, a selective dark outline (dark on the outer silhouette, lighter or coloured inside), small but well-constructed faces (clear eyes with a tiny highlight, brows, a hint of mouth), careful anatomy (well-shaped skulls and heads, believable hands), crisp edges, no anti-aliasing, no painterly blur. It must not look like cheap mobile pixel art.
   - About 72-80 art pixels tall per figure, each art pixel about 8-10 image pixels, square blocks.
   If the first result misses (wrong count, different scales, flat 2-tone shading, blank faces, wonky heads or anatomy, smooth/painterly instead of crisp pixels, flat front poses), redraw it, up to 3 attempts, and keep the best.
3. Save the chosen original untouched as out/raw.png and write out/NOTES.md: which attempt you kept and why, the prompt you used, and honest weaknesses. Do no keying, resizing or palette processing.
Work only inside this folder.
