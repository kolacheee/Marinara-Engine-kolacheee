# Five townsfolk

Left to right: ranger, smith, cleric, merchant, guard. All are grounded adults, drawn front-on at one shared scale with four-head proportions. Costumes use muted earth colours and restrained practical detail.

## Ranger — Osric Fen
A wiry man of about sixty with weathered olive skin, a long angular face, a bald crown, close grey side hair and a narrow grey moustache. He wears a short, square-cut charcoal rain cape over a faded clay-red tunic, dark umber trousers, calf wraps and scuffed boots; no hood, green cloak or bow. His single role prop is a compact wooden crossbow held horizontally at waist height. His narrow shoulders and straight-legged silhouette feel alert and spare.

## Smith — Marta Flint
A broad, powerful woman of forty with fair freckled skin, a square jaw, thick brows and short iron-grey hair cropped close at the sides. Her sleeves are rolled above strong forearms: a muted slate work shirt, a russet split leather apron ending above the knees, charcoal trousers and heavy square-toed boots. She holds one pair of long iron forging tongs pointing downward beside her leg. Her wide planted stance and solid rectangular torso distinguish her from the reference's bearded male smith.

## Cleric — Sava Reed
A slender woman of seventy with deep brown skin, prominent cheekbones, a composed lined face and short white coils visible beneath a simple close-fitting linen cap. A straight, ankle-length warm stone robe, narrow ochre stole and dark soft shoes give her a quiet column silhouette. She holds a small bronze sun-disc reliquary upright at her sternum with both hands; it is her single unmistakable sacred marker. The robe is plain, heavy and practical, with no jewels or aristocratic cape.

## Merchant — Belan Rusk
A stout man of thirty-five with warm medium-brown skin, full cheeks, a broad nose, a clean-shaven chin and dense black curls cut in a rounded crop. A faded plum sleeveless waistcoat over an oatmeal shirt, a broad olive sash, loose brown breeches and worn low shoes form a rounded silhouette. He carries one thick closed account ledger beneath his left arm, with its dull brass clasp facing forward. His expression is shrewd and approachable, with no hat, fine jewellery or noble dress.

## Guard — Tessa Vale
An athletic woman of twenty-eight with tawny skin, a long blunt-featured face, dark straight brows and black hair in a short jaw-length bob. She wears a muted mustard quilted jack, a plain dull-steel breastplate, a short grey-brown divided overskirt, dark leggings and sturdy boots. A single tall ash spear with a modest leaf-shaped steel head stands vertically beside her right shoulder; no shield or additional weapon. Her upright shoulders, tapering waist and balanced stance give her a disciplined silhouette without making her larger in scale than the others.
