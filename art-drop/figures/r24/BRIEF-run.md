# Round 24 test: five townsfolk drawn together in ONE image

1. Write fresh, specific designs for five townsfolk: a ranger, a smith, a cleric, a merchant and a guard. Grounded adult fantasy, muted earthy world, varied age, build, skin tone, hair, face and silhouette; each with one clear prop or role marker. Invent them now; there are no earlier designs to use. Save them to out/DESIGNS.md (a short paragraph each).
2. Make ONE image-tool call that draws all five side by side, front view, full body, standing on one shared ground line, at the same scale, on a flat magenta #FF00FF background, nothing else in the frame. Ask for crisp pixel art with one consistent pixel grid across the whole image, where each adult figure is roughly 90 art-pixels tall (art-pixels = the drawing's own pixel blocks), 4 heads tall, dark coloured outlines, 4-tone shading lit from the upper left. Attach style-ref/STYLE-SHEET.png as a STYLE-ONLY reference with this instruction: "Match this sheet's rendering: pixel cluster size, 4-tone shading, coloured contours, proportions, costume detail and craft. Do NOT copy its characters: the five people must look nothing like the ranger, blacksmith or noble on the sheet."
   If the first result is weak (figures at different scales, broken pixel grid, figures touching, wrong count), redraw it, up to 3 attempts, and keep the best.
3. Save the chosen original, untouched, as out/raw.png. Do no other processing: no keying, resizing or palette work (that is done separately). End with out/NOTES.md: which attempt you kept and why, and honest weaknesses.
Work only inside this folder. The only image you may attach is style-ref/STYLE-SHEET.png (plus your own attempts from this run).
