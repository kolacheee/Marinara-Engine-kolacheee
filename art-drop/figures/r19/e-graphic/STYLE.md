# Ember & Ink

A bold graphic storybook treatment for adult fantasy field characters. Use compact, roughly three-head figures, dark coloured contours, broad earthy colour planes, and a cool edge light. The costumes carry character identity; facial detail stays economical. The reference is `STYLE-SHEET.png`. The twelve transparent PNGs in `sprites/` are the authoritative finished assets. Do not sample working magenta or the paper background.

## Frame and proportions

- Canvas: **96 × 128 pixels**, RGBA, with alpha strictly 0 or 255.
- Visible figure, including hair and footwear: **92 pixels tall**, from row **33** through **124**, inclusive. All coordinates are zero-based. Rows 125–127 remain empty.
- Construction target: **three head units**, approximately 30.7 pixels each. Crown, chin, hip and sole are the four guide lines. Hair and beard volume can extend outside the anatomical divisions; do not count the beard as a whole extra head.
- Keep an adult jaw, a small nose projection, restrained expressions and recognisable adult clothes. The enlarged head is a field-sprite convention. Avoid baby cheeks and oversized sparkling eyes.
- A slender character's frontal body is roughly 25–34 pixels wide; Hadrik is broader. Weapons, hair, capes and elbows may extend the silhouette. Preserve empty frame margins.
- Place the centre of the planted footwear near x=48. Do not centre the whole silhouette around a longbow: that makes the body slide between views.
- Front and back are straight-on; side and walk-side face right. Do not horizontally flip finished art to invent another direction, because equipment and lighting have sides.

## Contours, clusters and faces

Use `#191B24` for the outer silhouette, normally **1 pixel**, increasing to **2 pixels** under hair, at soles and at important overlaps. Interior lines use a dark local ramp colour or `#30313C`. Pure black is not in the palette. Keep the outside contour continuous even beside the rim light; the rim belongs inside it.

An eye is a **2 × 2 or 2 × 3 dark block**, with a short 1-pixel eyebrow extending its top. In profile show one eye only. No sclera, eyelashes, individual irises or white eye glints. The nose projects 1–2 pixels; the mouth is a quiet 1–2-pixel warm mark. Use a broad skin plane with a small warm shadow under hair and chin. Hadrik's lit skin must remain dark brown, not share the pale-skin highlight.

Prefer contiguous clusters at least 2 × 2 pixels, and broad material planes 4–12 pixels across. One-pixel exceptions are contours, eye edges, bowstring, arrow tips and short rim accents. **No dithering, stippled texture or scattered grain.** Diagonals should form deliberate stepped runs. Do not resample the final sprites with a smooth display filter.

Hands are 5–8-pixel mitten shapes: one thumb notch, one lower shadow, at most one short finger division. A gripping hand must visibly interrupt the bow or hammer handle. Fingers are not separately outlined. Feet use chunky toe blocks, a dark sole and one broad upper plane.

## Lighting and materials

Use an upper-left key light, about 45 degrees down toward the figure. Keep it in image space for every view. Each material gets a shadow, a dominant base, and at most one broad lit plane. A narrow, discontinuous cool rim on the right shadow edge is a separate light cue, not a fourth broad shading band. Aim for a 1-pixel rim at this size, especially on hair, shoulder, outer cloth edge and boot toe. No bloom, transparent glow, gradient or cast ground shadow.

The sphere on the sheet demonstrates the indigo ramp: `#668298` key, `#344F70` base, `#202D49` shadow, `#ADC8C8` rim. Its stepped circular silhouette is a drawing from the image tool, reduced and palette-snapped by the same process as the sprites.

- **Pale skin:** `#E2AC83` dominant light, `#B96845` small warm shadow, with `#E9D8AD` used sparingly. Do not use gold as the principal skin colour.
- **Very dark skin:** `#4A2928` shadow, `#844335` lit/base plane. A small warm accent may use `#B96845`; keep most skin dark.
- **Auburn hair:** three large swept locks, `#844335`, `#B96845`, and a restrained warm accent. Wren's ponytail begins low behind the ear/nape and stays short enough to clear the quiver.
- **Black hair and beard:** `#191B24`, `#30313C`, with `#50515A` for a broad reflected plane. Hadrik's beard is one strong mass with a few large lower teeth, not hair-by-hair texture.
- **Golden hair:** old-gold ramp, one broad crown highlight and 2–3 large updo coils. Keep the updo above the short cape.
- **Cloth:** 2–3 broad folded planes. The green cloak has a large hanging mass and a folded-down hood. The Noble's long skirt has three main vertical planes at rest; the cape is short. No embroidery, lace or tiny repeated motifs.
- **Leather:** broad rust/brown planes with dark seams. Apron, jerkin and boots share colours but differ by shape. One buckle highlight is enough.
- **Metal:** dark compact shapes with a sharp light edge. Hadrik's hammer head uses the ink/iron ramp and a cool rim. Gold trim and clasps use the old-gold ramp; avoid repeated specular dots.
- **Wood:** the leather ramp, with one clear longbow curve or straight hammer handle. Wren's bowstring is a narrow pale line with transparent air around it.

## Character continuity

The bow is carried in her left hand, on the viewer's right in front view. Quiver and bow reverse their screen positions in back view. **Hadrik:** blacksmith in his forties; very dark skin; short black hair with a broad swept crown; full black beard; cream shirt with rolled sleeves; leather apron; dark trousers, heavy boots and compact iron hammer. His back view shows crossed apron straps and a waist tie. Preserve the contrast between dark skin and cream sleeves. The hammer stays in his left hand.

**Noble:** young adult woman; fair skin; golden updo; long deep-blue gown with plain gold hem, belt and cuff accents; short blue cape with gold edge. Use broad blue planes, not ornamental surface detail. Keep her hem near the ankles and show a small dark shoe at rest.

## Passing frame rules

The fourth frame is one **right-facing passing pose**, not a complete walk cycle. The supporting foot sits beneath the pelvis on row 124. The free knee bends and its shoe rises beside or behind the supporting calf. The legs pass close together, rather than forming a wide contact stride. The torso stays upright. Swing the free arm modestly against the leg motion and keep carried equipment in the same hand.

Wren's cloak trails slightly and the bow stays upright. Hadrik's apron remains heavy with little flare. The Noble reveals her lifted shoe under a raised portion of the hem; the skirt's broad planes tilt with the leg. Keep the head and costume volumes close to the standing model. A finished animation would additionally need contact, down, opposite passing and opposite contact frames, with explicit registration and timing checks.

## Exact reduction and cleanup recipe

The reproducible scripts live in `../scratch/build.py` and `../scratch/assemble_sheet.py`. They do not draw or repaint character pixels. Every figure began as an image-tool drawing; processing is keying, downscaling, palette snapping, registration, sheet assembly and checking.

1. Draw large on flat magenta `#FF00FF`, with empty gutters. Establish the three side models first. Retain the accepted original as `big/side-models.png`; use it as the image reference for each character's front/back/passing triptych. Retain those originals unchanged in `big/`. Failed proportion studies stay in `../scratch/`.
2. Split each source horizontally into three equal columns, using rounded column boundaries. Side-model columns are Wren, Hadrik, Noble. Each character triptych is front, back, walk-side.
3. Convert to RGB. Key a pixel if **R > 120, B > 120, R − G > 65 and B − G > 65**. This also removes magenta inside bow gaps and between limbs. Keyed pixels become `(0,0,0,0)`; other pixels start opaque. Do not erase pale cool rim pixels.
4. Crop to the nontransparent bounding box. Resize that crop to height **92**, with width `round(source_width × 92 / source_height)`, using Pillow **BOX** area reduction on RGBA. This averages the large drawing into stable small clusters; it is not nearest-neighbour sampling of the large input. RGBA resizing uses premultiplied coverage.
5. Make alpha binary at **128**: coverage below 128 becomes fully transparent; coverage of 128 or greater becomes fully opaque. Transparent RGB is zeroed.
6. Snap every retained RGB to the final 24-colour palette by minimizing **2(R−r)² + 3(G−g)² + 2(B−b)²**. No error diffusion or dithering. Palette order breaks exact ties. This is the only colour cleanup; there is no painted patch layer.
7. Tight-crop again. If thresholding removed an outer row, nearest-neighbour resize back to height 92 and snap again. Measure the alpha bounds in the last three rows to find the footwear centre. Place it at approximately x=48 in the 96×128 frame; clamp the horizontal origin only if needed to keep the figure inside. Set y to `125 − reduced_height`, so the lowest opaque row is 124.
8. Save RGBA PNG. Verify 96×128 dimensions, exactly 92 occupied rows, lowest opaque pixel on row 124, only alpha 0/255, all opaque RGB values in the master palette, and at most 24 opaque colours per sprite. The whole-cast colour union must not exceed 24. Results are in `../scratch/checks.json`.
9. Review every sprite at native size and with nearest-neighbour enlargement. Check the eyes, bowstring, tool grip, feet, limb gaps and consistency of costume. Regenerate a weak drawing from the accepted side reference; do not silently invent missing anatomy in code. For this sheet, the proportion studies were redrawn before view expansion and the pale-skin palette slot was corrected after reduction.
10. Assemble the neutral-paper sheet at 2040×2600. Display every full 96×128 frame at **exactly 3× nearest neighbour**. The guide figure, detail crops, rule swatches and shading sphere are additional illustrations. Typography and layout colours are not part of the sprite palette. Inspect the full sheet and native-resolution detail tiles.

Rebuild from this project folder with `python scratch/build.py` followed by `python scratch/assemble_sheet.py`. The original drawings are required. The scripts operate only on paths inside this folder.
