# Round 25: five original characters in ONE image, HD-2D field-sprite conventions

1. Invent five ORIGINAL characters with new names and designs: a traveling merchant with a big pack, a scholar in a long coat, a dancer, a hunter with a cloak, and a young cleric with a tall staff. Stylised and slightly heroic even though they are townsfolk. No existing game's characters, art or names; nothing from earlier work. Write out/DESIGNS.md (a short paragraph each).
2. Make ONE image-tool call that draws all five side by side on a flat #FF00FF background, one shared ground line, same scale, nothing else in the frame. No reference images attached. Target look (describe it in the prompt):
   - About 2.5-3 heads tall: big head, large stylised hair with clear clumps, tiny simple face (eyes a few dark pixels, no nose detail), slim tapering legs, small feet.
   - Bold, simple, readable silhouettes with flowing elements that hang: capes, long coat tails, scarves, sashes.
   - Strong 1-art-pixel dark outline, saturated accent colours, 3-4 tone cel shading lit from the upper left.
   - Low art-pixel count: each figure about 48 art pixels tall, drawn LARGE with chunky square blocks (each art pixel about 12-16 image pixels), crisp, no anti-aliasing.
   - Front three-quarter facing (slightly turned toward the viewer's left or right), not a flat mugshot front.
   If the first result misses (wrong count, figures at different scales, smooth/painterly instead of chunky pixels, flat front poses), redraw it, up to 3 attempts, and keep the best.
3. Save the chosen original untouched as out/raw.png and write out/NOTES.md: which attempt you kept and why, the prompt you used, and honest weaknesses. Do no keying, resizing or palette processing.
Work only inside this folder.
