Cloisonne uses violet-black #1c1222 ink, closed colour fields, jewel garments, large heads, the supplied face grid, and broad top-lit planes without gradients, dithering or antialiasing. `ramps.json` copies the brief exactly: skin, hair and cloth A have 4 shades; iris and cloth B have 3; fixed leather, metal and horn have 4, wood, gem and underclothes have 3, and eye white and ink have 1 each, with all requested target palettes included. Everything was drawn natively in Python/Pillow from character grids and polygon pieces in `tools/figs.py`, with no image tool or existing artwork; `tools/compose.py` performs exact indexed recolouring and the specified layer stacking, and `tools/check.py` audits the results. The 16 sheets, four PNG proof plates and looping 150 ms GIF were inspected enlarged, including every sheet at 4x and 6x, all six characters in every direction, recolour swatches, silhouettes and the scratch cobble plate. The final audit passes palette, binary alpha, face-template, dimensions, planted feet, leg gap, bob, mirror, source-region, closed-edge, visible-feature and exposed front-fist checks; no ink 2x2 blocks remain outside soles, no underclothes show in the tested composites, and every layer is below 6% lone pixels. Weaknesses remain: the bodies share one height, the long hair is rather rigid and reduces the ranger's silhouette distinction, mage profiles and sash ends are blocky, and symmetric frontal light provides limited modelling. Small shine and crease details are not perfectly bilateral, and shade-area percentages have not been proved for every connected field. Hair versus ears, cuffs versus fists and hat versus hair required repeated overlap corrections; the staff is shoulder-high so its finial does not hide either elf ear, and front hair locks end sooner than the back to expose the hands. Two literal brief checks cannot both coexist with the more specific geometry: the required 1 px downward bob makes moving naked bodies 42 px tall rather than 43, and outlined 3-row feet necessarily contain a 1 px colour row between ink. Narrow cuffs, forearms, ties and the staff also trigger that blanket narrow-cell rule; all such hits are enumerated in `scratch/check-report.json`, not claimed as passing it. This is therefore a complete sample delivery with documented stylistic exceptions, not a claim of literal compliance with every constraint.
