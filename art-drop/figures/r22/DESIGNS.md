## Shared production notes

These are quiet, grounded figures with supported weight, compact anatomy and muted materials. Adult height notes describe relative anatomy and proportions within the same 92 occupied rows, not different export sizes. Adults use approximately four 23 px head units, crowns at row 33 and lowest planted soles at row 124 of a 96 × 128 transparent frame. Davi occupies about 70 rows, crown near row 55, with the same sole row. Centre the complete equipped silhouette. Broad body proportions, garment boundaries and negative spaces carry identity; embroidery and tiny accessories do not.

Only one additional ramp is used anywhere in this set: **plum**, a subdued brown-violet cloth ramp, with edge **#352A32**, shadow **#51404C**, body **#766071**, light **#A08C9A**. Every palette below is exhaustive: named dominant and secondary ramps plus exactly the named skin ramp. Each has six ramps, hence at most 24 colours, leaving no need to exceed the 32-colour limit. Ramp names describe colour families, not compulsory materials: gold cloth is dull ochre, steel hair is grey, and leather-coloured cloth remains cloth. Metal is always steel. Use charcoal darks for dark eye marks; never pure black or white.

Faces use three to five large, listed cues, a one-pixel profile nose projection, dark eye marks without glints and a quiet mouth shadow. Render hair in three to five substantial locks, with no fine strand texture. Clothing has few gravity-led folds, thick matte hems and clear local-colour outlines. Upper-left lighting stays fixed in screen space. Hands are small mitten masses with closed, legible grips. Right and left below always mean the character's anatomical hands; equipment stays assigned when views change. All image prompts describe the initial full-body right-facing side view. Subsequent front views must retain the structural hooks described below; equipment cannot be the sole distinguishing feature. Town walking uses short heel-to-toe strides, supported passing poses and restrained arm swings.
