## Gutterlight (noir) - batch 2: re-ink the city as true comic art, plus a game stage
## House style for every picture below (the batch-1 results came out painted/airbrushed; these must NOT): an ORIGINAL printed comic-book panel, INKED, not painted. Thick black brush-ink contour lines of varying weight, huge solid SPOT BLACKS filling shadows and whole building faces, cross-hatching and parallel pen hatching for mid-tones, FLAT limited colour fills (no gradients, no airbrush, no soft glow, no bloom, no blur, no photographic lighting, no depth-of-field), visible coarse Ben-Day halftone dots for tints, slight off-register print feel on cheap newsprint. Rain drawn as straight slanted ink strokes. A gothic-industrial city that never existed. Palette strictly limited: ink #0b0b0d, newsprint/smog greys, sodium amber #e9a83a, ONE cold teal #3fd6c6 accent; NO red, NO pink, NO neon rings or circles anywhere. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers or readable signs; no logos or emblems; no bats or bat-wing shapes; no searchlights or beams on clouds; no costumed figures; no recognisable real or fictional landmarks.
