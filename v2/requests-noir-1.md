## Gutterlight (noir) - batch 1: backdrops and key art
## House style for every picture below: an ORIGINAL inked comic-book illustration. Bold black brush-ink linework and big spotted blacks, flat muted colour, subtle halftone dot shading, rain drawn as fine slanted ink strokes. A gothic-industrial city that never existed. Absolutely no text, letters, numbers or readable signs anywhere; no logos or emblems; no bats or bat-wing shapes; no searchlights or beams projected on clouds; no costumed figures; no recognisable real or fictional landmarks.
