## Copperplate Engine, batch 1 (backdrops and key art first)
## Look for every picture: a hand-tinted Victorian copperplate ENGRAVING (fine cross-hatched ink line work, stippled shading, like a plate from an 1870s scientific journal), lightly watercoloured. NOT a glossy 3D render, NOT brown gears everywhere. Palette: card cream #f3ead6, ink #2b2118, polished brass #c9a04a / #e3c378, copper #b8693d, verdigris #4f9a8a, Prussian iron blue #26354a, oxblood #8e2b22 used sparingly.
