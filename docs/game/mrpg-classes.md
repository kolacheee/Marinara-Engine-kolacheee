# Game Mode: MRPG Classes

Every new game plays on **MRPG** (Marinara RPG) classes, including games made from an Experience. Each party member has a class, levels up from fights, and unlocks new classes as they grow. Games made before MRPG classes existed keep their **Legacy** sheets until you choose to move them over (see [Move a Legacy game to MRPG](#move-a-legacy-game-to-mrpg)).

A game played with a ruleset, such as 5e (SRD 5.1), uses the ruleset's own sheets instead, and MRPG classes do not apply to it.

## Classes and trees

There are eight trees: **Blade**, **Guard**, **Scout**, **Marksman**, **Channeler**, **Hexer**, **Mender** and **Herald**. Each character trains in two of them:

- **Primary tree.** Chosen from their sheet. The **Game Mode** section of a library character or persona card can pick it. Otherwise it is read from the character's class and abilities.
- **Secondary tree.** Opened by the temperament the character starts the game with. A character whose temperament points at their own primary tree trains it alone, and can reach classes only open to someone who stays in one tree.

Every tree starts in a base class. A class promotes at class level 10 and again at level 20, and levels stop at 30. A character can carry up to four skills from other classes they have trained in. Classes are switched outside battle, from the character sheet. Stats follow the active class.

## The character sheet

Open a party member's sheet from the party list. It has four tabs: **Status**, **Classes**, **Skills** and **Gear**. Growth rates are hidden. With **Debug** on, the exact growth percentages show.

Your party levels up from fights the party wins. A lost or fled fight gives no experience. With **Permadeath** on (a setup option), a party member who falls in a fight and is not saved before it ends is gone for good.

## Skill checks

Checks outside battle read the character's MRPG stats. See [Dice and Skill Checks](dice-and-skill-checks.md#how-the-result-is-decided) for which stat each skill uses.

## Newcomers to the party

Someone who joins after the game began comes in at the level the Game Master gives them. Most newcomers start low, and a seasoned fighter may start near the party's level. The Game Master can also give a newcomer:

- **Faster growth** in some stats, for a raw talent who will catch up quickly.
- **Better stats from the start**, for someone already decent.

The Game Master decides this when the character joins. A newcomer it says nothing about starts at level 1.

## Playing with others

In a shared game, each guest builds their own character when they join the room: a name, a description, a **character concept** for the Game Master, and the same **Game Mode** picks a persona has (starting tree, strong stats and weak stat). Choosing one of your personas fills these in from it. The concept is where you tell the Game Master who your character is and how you would like them to come into the story.

The Game Master then fits the character into the story and picks their sister tree.

- **A new game.** Everyone, host and guests, starts at level 1.
- **A game already under way.** The Game Master also decides the guest's starting level, and can give them faster growth or better stats, as for any [newcomer](#newcomers-to-the-party).
- **A returning guest.** A guest's character stays in the game when they leave, with its level, gear and tamed beasts. While their player is away, the character sits out fights and earns no experience, and the Game Master leaves them out of scenes. When the guest comes back, they play the same character at the level they left it.

## Move a Legacy game to MRPG

A Legacy game keeps its Legacy sheets for as long as you want. To move it over, open the **Journal**, and on the **All** tab find the **MRPG classes** card. Select **Upgrade**.

For each party member, the card shows:

- **Class.** The tree they start in, filled in from their library card's pick, or else from their Legacy class. You can change it.
- **Sister tree.** The secondary tree their temperament opens. A character with no temperament yet gets one read from their Description when you upgrade.
- **Gear.** Each Legacy weapon and piece of armour they carry, and the MRPG piece it would become. Clear the box to leave one as it is.

When you confirm with **Upgrade the party**:

- Every party member starts at level 10, the top of their starting class, with each level's stats rolled as a level-up would.
- Their card's class becomes their MRPG class. Their Legacy class and abilities move into a **Before the upgrade** note on the card, so the story still knows them. Their Legacy stats also stay on the card.
- Each accepted weapon or piece of armour becomes its MRPG piece and keeps its name, and they wear it if their class can.

> **Warning:** The upgrade is one-way. A game that moved to MRPG classes can't go back to Legacy sheets.

The upgrade is not offered during a fight. Finish the fight first. A game played with a ruleset can't be upgraded: it keeps its ruleset's sheets.
